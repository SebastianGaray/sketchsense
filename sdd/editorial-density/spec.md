# Editorial density specification

- Remove visible copy that repeats controls, adjacent headings, or evidence.
- Replace promotional or value-loaded labels with factual language.
- Preserve limitations, safety boundaries, metrics, accessibility labels, and actionable states.
- Keep English and Spanish interfaces behaviorally aligned where both are available.

## Acceptance criteria

- Primary pages communicate purpose without repeated instructions or defensive prose.
- Technical evidence remains specific and inspectable.
- Existing navigation and application behavior remain unchanged.
